MindCare Therapy

A modern, compassionate WordPress website for a mental health therapy practice optimized for local search.

The Challenge

MindCare Therapy had an outdated website that loaded slowly on mobile (PageSpeed 34/100) and was not appearing in local search results when patients searched for therapists in their area.

The Solution

Built a bespoke lightweight WordPress theme, structured dedicated treatment pages targeting high-intent keywords, added appointment scheduling widgets, and optimized Google Business Profile.
