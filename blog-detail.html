How Mental Health Websites Can Rank Higher on Google

May 12, 2026 • 5 min read

SEO for healthcare and counseling practices requires establishing E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness). Google applies extra scrutiny to websites under the Your Money Your Life (YMYL) category.

1. Implement Medical & Local Schema Markup

Structured data (JSON-LD) tells search engines exactly who you are, what credentials your practice holds, and where your physical office is located. Adding MedicalBusiness and Physician schema is crucial.

2. Optimize for Local Intent Keywords

Most patients search for "therapist near me" or "anxiety counselor in [City]". Ensure each location has a dedicated, keyword-targeted landing page with embedded Google Maps and client reviews.

3. Focus on Page Speed & Mobile UX

If your website takes more than 3 seconds to load on mobile phones, users seeking urgent consultation will press back. Fast loading speeds directly improve conversion rates.
