FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about my development and SEO workflow.

How long does a web development project take to complete?
Standard business websites typically take 2 to 3 weeks from kickoff to launch. Custom web applications, complex platforms, or e-commerce implementations take 4 to 6 weeks.
How quickly will my site see SEO ranking improvements on Google?
Technical fixes, speed enhancements, and local SEO optimizations often generate initial indexation and ranking improvements within 30 to 60 days. Highly competitive keyword rankings generally reach Page #1 within 3 to 6 months.
Can you work with my existing WordPress, Shopify, or Webflow website?
Yes! I regularly audit, optimize, rebuild, and scale existing websites on platforms including WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, and custom HTML/React tech stacks.
Do you offer ongoing website maintenance and technical SEO support?
Yes! I provide monthly maintenance packages including server security monitoring, Core Web Vitals performance checks, plugin updates, automated backups, and detailed monthly SEO rank tracking.
